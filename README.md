# Temperature-variable-standof
# US Patent 11,324,110 — Temperature-Variable Standoff for Circuit Boards

**Inventor:** Rama Kumar Mayil (co-inventor)
**Assignee:** Honeywell International Inc.
**Issued:** May 3, 2022
**Filed:** April 28, 2020 (priority date March 3, 2020, via Indian Provisional Application 202011009058)
**Status:** Active, expires September 4, 2040

## Overview

This patent covers a standoff structure used to mechanically support and space apart circuit boards inside an electronics assembly — while also actively heating or cooling a specific area of the board. Traditional standoffs are purely mechanical; this design embeds a temperature-variable electrical element (a resistive heater or thermoelectric cooler) directly into the standoff itself, so it can regulate the temperature of nearby components without adding separate hardware.

## Why it matters

Electronics used in extreme environments (aerospace, defense, telecom infrastructure) often have temperature-sensitive components, like SIM cards or ICs, that need to stay within a safe operating range even when the surrounding environment swings from -55°C to well above normal operating temperatures. This invention lets the mounting hardware double as a compact, integrated climate-control system for that specific zone of the board, improving reliability without major added size, weight, or cost.
